## Verified compliant

- PS/AS/resource revocation endpoints are available and advertised when configured: PS and AS map role-scoped revocation endpoints and metadata (`src/AAuth/Person/AAuthPersonServerEndpoints.cs:169`, `src/AAuth/Person/AAuthPersonServerEndpoints.cs:200`, `src/AAuth/Access/AAuthAccessServerEndpoints.cs:123`, `src/AAuth/Access/AAuthAccessServerEndpoints.cs:148`); resource metadata exposes an optional `revocation_endpoint` (`src/AAuth/DependencyInjection/AAuthResourceOptions.cs:117`).
- Revocation request is a signed POST and requires body coverage for `content-digest` and `content-type` — role middleware requires those components and the endpoint double-checks them (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:57`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:133`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:145`).
- Revocation client produces `{ "jti", "exp" }` JSON and requests content coverage (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationClient.cs:40`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationClient.cs:44`), matching required body members (#token-revocation, L2687–L2688).
- Recipient derives issuer from the verified signer, rejects non-server signing identities or unaccepted issuers as `unsupported_iss`, and keys revocation by `(verified caller, jti)` (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:148`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:152`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:179`).
- Malformed/missing `jti` or `exp`, excessive future `exp`, and rate-limit responses map to the specified status codes and include `Retry-After` for `rate_limited` (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:157`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:174`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:176`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:181`; `src/AAuth/Errors/RevocationError.cs:33`, `src/AAuth/Errors/RevocationError.cs:42`).
- No not-found oracle on POST: after recording an unseen or seen `(iss,jti)`, the endpoint returns `200 OK` when there is no downstream work (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:186`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:197`; #why-revocation-no-not-found, L3534).
- In-memory retention and bounds are present: revocations are retained until expiration plus store retention, future `exp` is bounded by `MaxTokenLifetime`, and per-issuer entry/rate limits are enabled by default (`src/AAuth/Server/InMemoryJtiStore.cs:73`, `src/AAuth/Server/InMemoryJtiStore.cs:100`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthRevocationOptions.cs:33`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationLimits.cs:13`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationLimits.cs:58`).
- Deferred revocation behavior verifies the polling identity, answers `404` to other callers, honors `Prefer: wait` up to a cap, and does not return `202` when there is no downstream work (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:197`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:210`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:214`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:245`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:259`).
- `200` revocation bodies use an optional JSON-object `downstream` array; clients parse absent bodies as success and malformed non-empty bodies as `revocation_unavailable` (`src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:226`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationEndpoint.cs:236`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationClient.cs:72`, `src/AAuth/Server/RevocationClient.cs:95`).
- Cascade graph records and walks issued grants, cascades PS-to-AS person-token revocations for four-party grants, revokes own auth/person tokens at their resources, and exposes downstream `revocation_unsupported`/`revocation_unavailable` outcomes (`src/AAuth/Server/AAuthRevocationService.cs:199`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthRevocationService.cs:204`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthRevocationService.cs:209`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthRevocationService.cs:216`, `src/AAuth/Errors/RevocationError.cs:63`).
- Agent-token revocation cascade is by recorded agent subject, not by just the one token jti: accepted agent tokens record subject, and `StartsAsync` adds the subject index (`src/AAuth/Person/AAuthPersonServerEndpoints.cs:404`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthRevocationService.cs:176`).
- Fresh token requests reject revoked parameter tokens with `revoked_<parameter>_token` and revoked Signature-Key tokens with `revoked_jwt` through `SourceRevoked` (`src/AAuth/Server/TokenRegistration.cs:18`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthProblemDetails.cs:23`, `src/AAuth/Server/AAuthProblemDetails.cs:32`).
- Revoked resource tokens cannot mint auth tokens at PS or AS once revocation is locally recorded (`src/AAuth/Person/AAuthPersonServerEndpoints.cs:375`, `src/AAuth/Access/AAuthAccessServerEndpoints.cs:313`; #revocation-cascade, L2753).
- Signature-Key token revocation is checked after JWT/trust verification and before accepting/registering the token, returning `Signature-Error: error=revoked_jwt` (`src/AAuth/Server/Verification/AAuthVerificationMiddleware.cs:230`, `src/AAuth/Server/Verification/AAuthVerificationMiddleware.cs:266`, `src/AAuth/Server/Verification/AAuthVerificationMiddleware.cs:400`; #verification, L2248).
