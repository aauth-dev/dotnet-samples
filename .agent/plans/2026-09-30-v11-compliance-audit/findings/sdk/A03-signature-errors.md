## Verified compliant

- `Signature-Error` producer uses RFC9651 dictionary shape `error=<token>` and `required_input=("...")` only for `invalid_input` — `src/AAuth/Errors/SignatureError.cs:74-80` vs (#header-structure, L1888-L1895) and (#invalid_input, L2025-L2034).
- `Signature-Error` consumer treats the header, not the body, as machine-readable input for adaptive signing — `src/AAuth/Agent/ChallengeHandler.cs:429-449` vs (#signature-error, L1909-L1911) and protocol (#authentication-errors, L2569).
- Missing `Signature`, `Signature-Input`, or `Signature-Key` maps to `invalid_signature` and is written through the 401 failure path — `src/AAuth/Server/Verification/AAuthVerificationMiddleware.cs:93-98`, `:400-404` vs (#verification, L2244) and (#why-401-signature-failures, L3526).
- Verification-step unsupported schemes are rejected deterministically with `unsupported_scheme` and `Accept-Signature-Scheme` — `src/AAuth/Server/Verification/AAuthVerificationMiddleware.cs:111-113`, `:405-408` vs (#verification, L2247) and (#unsupported_scheme, L1980-L1987).
- Unsupported/absent/polymorphic key algorithms are rejected as `unsupported_algorithm`, with `Accept-Signature-Alg: Ed25519, ES256` — `src/AAuth/Crypto/KeyFactory.cs:44-48`, `src/AAuth/Server/Verification/AAuthVerificationMiddleware.cs:405-406` vs (#algorithm-selection, L2533-L2543).
- `keyid` conflicts are rejected and verification takes the key from `Signature-Key` — `src/AAuth/HttpSig/AAuthVerifier.cs:71-73` vs (#accept-signature, L1616-L1619).
- JWKS unknown-key handling re-fetches through cache miss before returning null, then maps to `unknown_key` — `src/AAuth/Discovery/JwksClient.cs:104-106`, `src/AAuth/HttpSig/DefaultSignatureKeyResolver.cs:147-154` vs (#unknown_key, L2053-L2056).
- The agent does not downgrade to `hwk` from a hostile `Accept-Signature-Scheme` response; `ChallengeHandler` ignores `Accept-Signature-Scheme`/`Accept-Signature-Alg` and only retries on `invalid_input` `required_input`, which is allowed because clients MAY ignore those headers — `src/AAuth/Agent/ChallengeHandler.cs:429-449` vs (#client-processing, L1830-L1837).
- Ordinary AAuth `AAuth-Requirement` challenges do not emit `Accept-Signature-*` (`AAuthChallengeMiddleware.cs:105-107`, `:129-130`, `:214-216`), but the assigned Signature Keys challenge text says the headers MAY be sent and are useful, not MUST/SHOULD, so I did not file this as a defect — (#sending-on-errors-and-challenges, L1647-L1656).
